# Lecture-grounded question writing

The user-supplied NB 06 Study Buddy examples calibrate clarity, discriminating
clues, one focused endpoint, and nearby misconception distractors. They are not
official school items, medical source evidence, imported questions, or learner
attempts. Only relevant constructions enter the prompt (at most two). ExamSoft
and IMCQ remain school-style references; objectives set scope and lecture facts
support correctness.

All MCQ generation paths and the independent reviewer share the writing standard.
An advanced label requires the reviewer to explicitly verify a connected chain,
one requested endpoint, and absence of a choice shortcut, plus necessary distinct
steps and literal supporting source excerpts. Earlier saved questions remain
stored, but new preparation requires the current review. Resumed older questions
still contribute ordinary answer evidence, without new advanced-reasoning credit.

Keyword overlap remains a fallback objective screen. An explicit semantic
alignment verdict may supersede it only for a structurally valid item with a
current source-checked reasoning audit, one real objective ID, and no blocking
review issues. This avoids rejecting cellular CSF findings solely because a broad
objective uses the word "composition".

Adaptive order quotas follow the allocator's per-objective target. Verified
application success may unlock supported integration; recent misses return the
objective to fresh application. Question count is not a mastery guarantee.

Lecture Quiz and Exam Practice share concise explanations and collapsed full
choice notes. A selected incorrect choice gets a visible contrast. Guided Tutor
keeps the teaching reply and one follow-up visible, while diagnostic notes and
card drafts remain inspectable behind disclosures.

The NB 06 live validation produced two accepted second-order examples across
generation and repair review. It did not establish genuine third-order output;
shortcut-based candidates were withheld. Passing unit tests or a model review
does not certify equivalence to an official NBME item.
